## 6. Current Situation & Google Verification Mechanism

### 6.1 The Verification Requirement: Live Video Verification
In recent compliance updates for sports training centers, physical academies, and local businesses in India, Google Business Profile routinely enforces **Live Video Verification** or **Video Recording Verification** to curb fraudulent listings.

During this process, an authorized academy representative uses a mobile smartphone to record or stream an uninterrupted, continuous video verifying three critical elements:
1. **Location & Exterior:** Showing the street signs, surrounding buildings, and exterior entrance signage displaying the academy name.
2. **Business Equipment & Courts:** Walking inside the arena to film the actual badminton courts, BWF synthetic mats, nets, coaching equipment, and changing facilities.
3. **Proof of Management / Access:** Showing access to staff-only areas, the management desk, equipment locker, or keys unlocking the facility.
