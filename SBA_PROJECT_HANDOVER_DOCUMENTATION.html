08 Admin Dashboard & Operations Platform

The management interface at /admin empowers non-technical staff to control academy operations, student enquiries, coaches, tournaments, and content without code changes:

1. Inquiries CRM & Drawer Live

Real-time lead list with status filtering (NEW, CONTACTED, TRIAL_SCHEDULED, ENROLLED, DROPPED). Clicking a student opens a slide-over drawer to add internal notes, click-to-dial, and launch pre-filled WhatsApp chats.

2. Training Centres CMS Live

Add/edit training facilities, update physical addresses, alter court counts, adjust morning/evening opening hours, update Google Maps links, and toggle published status.

3. Coaches & Faculty Live

Manage coaching staff, upload portrait headshots, record BWF/NIS certifications, input years of experience, assign primary training venue, and publish profiles.

4. Photo Gallery Manager Live

Direct image upload system with relational database binary storage. Assign photos to Training, Courts, or Tournaments with captions and delete functionality.

5. Articles & Blog CMS Live

Publish coaching advice, footwork tutorials, and tournament summaries with automatic URL slugs, reading times, tags, and SEO meta tags.

6. Trophy Wall & Laurels Live

Record state/district tournament medals (Gold, Silver, Bronze), championship names, age categories, and student honorees to update the live trophy wall.

7. Customer Reviews Live

Moderate student and parent reviews, adjust star ratings (1 to 5), edit copy for typos, and toggle approval flags for display on the public testimonial slider.

8. Events & Tournaments Live

Create sanctioned championships, holiday coaching camps, entry fees, start dates, and external registration links; automatically feeds dynamic sitemap.

Staff & User Management (RBAC)
Administrators can manage staff accounts inside PostgreSQL users with role-based permissions (SUPER_ADMIN, ADMIN, COACH, FRONT_DESK, CONTENT_MANAGER) to prevent unauthorized access to financial or customer records.

Sooraj Badminton Academy Authentication & Database Architecture

09 Authentication & Authorization Security

Password Hashing Engine

Utilizes Node.js native scrypt cryptographic key derivation with a cryptographically secure 16-byte random salt. Verification employs crypto.timingSafeEqual to prevent timing attack enumeration.

{16-byte-salt}:{64-byte-scrypt-derived-key}
JWT Sessions & Rotation

Access tokens signed with HMAC-SHA256 (15-min lifespan). Refresh tokens (30-day lifespan) stored in PostgreSQL with SHA-256 token hashing, rotation upon use, and automatic reuse attack revocation.

HttpOnly; SameSite=Lax; Secure (Production)

Sooraj Badminton Academy Administrator User Guide

20 Step-by-Step Administrator User Guide

Practical, numbered instructions for academy directors and front-desk staff to manage daily operations:

Workflow 1: Sign in to Dashboard
  1. Navigate to: https://soorajbadmintonacademy.in/admin
  2. Enter your registered administrative email and password.
  3. Click Sign In to Dashboard.
  4. The system redirects to the live operations overview.
Workflow 2: Manage Student Enquiries
  1. Click Student Inquiries in the left sidebar.
  2. Filter by status (New, Contacted, Trial Booked).
  3. Click any lead row to open the Lead Drawer.
  4. Click Call or WhatsApp to contact the parent.
  5. Update the lead status and add internal notes.
Workflow 3: Update Training Centres
  1. Click Centres & Hubs in the left sidebar.
  2. Click Edit Centre on the venue to modify.
  3. Update address, court counts, operating hours, or maps URL.
  4. Click Save Changes to instantly update the public site.
Workflow 4: Add or Edit Coaches
  1. Click Coaches & Staff in the left sidebar.
  2. Click Add New Coach or click an existing coach card.
  3. Enter name, certifications (BWF/NIS), and experience.
  4. Upload a portrait photo and click Publish Coach.
Workflow 5: Upload Photos to Gallery
  1. Click Photo Gallery in the left sidebar.
  2. Click Upload New Photo and choose an image.
  3. Enter a caption (e.g., "Junior Batch at Kadavanthra").
  4. Select category (Training, Courts, Tournaments) and save.
Workflow 6: Publish Blog Articles
  1. Click Blog Articles in the left sidebar.
  2. Click Create New Article.
  3. Enter the title, summary, markdown body, and topic tags.
  4. Click Publish Article to update /blog.
Workflow 7: Log Tournament Medals
  1. Click Track Record & Awards in the left sidebar.
  2. Click Add Achievement.
  3. Enter tournament name, student name, year, and medal type.
  4. Click Save Record to update the live Trophy Wall.
Workflow 8: Change Admin Password
  1. Click Password at the bottom of the sidebar.
  2. Enter your current password and your new secure password.
  3. Click Update Password.
