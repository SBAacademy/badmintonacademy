## 1. Metadata Generation & Canonical Strategy

### Global Configuration (`app/layout.tsx`)
* **Metadata Base:** Configured dynamically:
  ```ts
  metadataBase: new URL(process.env.NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL || "https://sabadmintonacademy.com")
  ```
* **Title Template:** Configured with `%s | S & A Badminton Academy`:
  - Child pages with titles like `Badminton Coaching in Kadavanthra` automatically render as `Badminton Coaching in Kadavanthra | S & A Badminton Academy`.
* **Global OpenGraph & Twitter:**
  - OG Type: `website`
  - Locale: `en_IN`
  - Image: 1200x630 high-res arena photography with explicit dimensions.
  - Twitter Card: `summary_large_image`.

### Page-Level Canonicals
Every page inspected utilizes `lib/seo.ts:createMetadata()` to enforce **self-referential canonical URLs**:
* `/` → `https://sabadmintonacademy.com`
* `/kadavanthra` → `https://sabadmintonacademy.com/kadavanthra`
* `/kumbalanghi` → `https://sabadmintonacademy.com/kumbalanghi`
* `/maradu` → `https://sabadmintonacademy.com/maradu`
* `/palluruthy` → `https://sabadmintonacademy.com/palluruthy`
* `/chullikal` → `https://sabadmintonacademy.com/chullikal`
* `/programs/kids-badminton-coaching` → `https://sabadmintonacademy.com/programs/kids-badminton-coaching`
* **Assessment:** Clean; no mixed canonicals, no duplicate trailing-slash conflicts.
