Project Overview & Business Objectives

This Commercial Proposal & Service Agreement ("Agreement") outlines the understanding between SBA BADMINTON ACADEMY ("Client") and KREOVATE ("Service Provider") for the design, development, implementation, and delivery of the Client's website and the agreed 30-day Local SEO services. Mohammed Nibrasul Haqq (Kreovate -Marketing Agency) will act as the designated project contact and delivery lead for KREOVATE.

Current Situation

SBA BADMINTON ACADEMY currently operates without a dedicated, high-performance website. This absence restricts search engine discoverability, limits structured presentation of coaching programs across branches, and leaves prospective students and parents relying on fragmented online mentions.

Proposed Solution

Deploy a modern, conversion-focused, SEO-ready website combined with an intensive 30-Day Local SEO launch. This comprehensive solution establishes a credible digital home while directly optimizing Google search visibility and local relevance for badminton coaching enquiries.

Seven Core Business Goals

1. Digital Authority: Launch an official web platform for SBA Badminton Academy.
2. Local Discoverability: Appear in local search queries for badminton coaching.
3. Search Relevance: Improve local relevance through geo-targeted on-page SEO.
4. Program Clarity: Showcase coaching batches, camps, court amenities, and timings.
5. Frictionless Enquiries: Direct parents to instant WhatsApp, direct phone calls, and forms.
6. Technical Search Setup: Configure Search Console, Analytics 4, and Schema markup.
7. Long-Term Foundation: Structured URLs ready for sustainable organic search growth.
"The website becomes the academy's digital foundation, while Local SEO helps make that foundation discoverable to people actively searching for badminton coaching and related services."

30-Day Local SEO Roadmap
Implementation Schedule

30-Day Local SEO Launch Plan

01
PHASE 1: SEO Research & Foundation Days 1 – 7

Conduct website structure review, competitor search visibility audit, local keyword research and search intent mapping (e.g. "Badminton coaching near me", "Badminton Academy in [Location]", "Kids badminton classes"), NAP content consistency review, and technical SEO planning.

02
PHASE 2: Website & On-Page SEO Deployment Days 8 – 15

Execute keyword-focused page titles and meta descriptions, configure structured H1/H2/H3 headings, implement internal linking between coaching programs and locations, optimize images with descriptive ALT tags, configure canonical tags, and publish location-specific landing content.

03
PHASE 3: Technical & Local Website Optimization Days 16 – 23

Execute technical SEO checks, indexability checks, XML sitemap generation, robots.txt directives, canonical configuration, LocalBusiness structured data deployment, mobile responsiveness checks, and page speed optimization.

04
PHASE 4: Measurement, Optimization & Recommendations Days 24 – 30

Monitor organic search visibility, verify website indexing status across primary pages, track target keywords, conduct on-page quality review, compile a Month-1 SEO Summary Report, and provide ongoing organic growth recommendations.

Note: Keyword examples illustrate target search intent. Search engine rankings depend on competition, historical authority, and location dynamics, and are not promised as guaranteed ranking positions.
